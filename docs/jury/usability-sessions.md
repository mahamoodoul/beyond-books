# Usability sessions (evidence for "Design")

Three people who have never seen the app each do the core task alone. You watch, you do not
help. Each session takes about 15 minutes. Teammates do not count; use friends, other teams,
or anyone at the venue.

## Setup

- A laptop with the app running and a chapter PDF ready on the desktop (one of
  `sample-courses/`, or any textbook chapter).
- A fresh teacher account is easiest: the login page offers quick sign-in in development mode.
- Say only this, then stay quiet:

> "You are a teacher. Make a course from this PDF, let the AI build a lesson with a 3D model,
> publish it, then look at it the way a student would."

## What to note while watching

- Where they paused for more than ten seconds, and what they were looking for.
- Anything they clicked that did not do what they expected.
- Any word on screen they asked about.
- Time to finish, and whether they finished without help. If you had to help, note where.
